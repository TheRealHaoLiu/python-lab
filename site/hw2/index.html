Variables Lab

A variable is a label stuck on a value. fred = 200 sticks the label fred on the number 200; after that, fred means 200 wherever you write it. Values come in types: whole numbers, decimals, and text.

= Labels

fred = 200 john = fred print(john)
  • = means “label this”, not “equals”. The name goes on the left, the value on the right.
  • john = fred copies the value: now both labels say 200.
  • print(fred) shows the value. print("fred") shows the word.

3.1 Types

20 3.1416 "Ada" "20"
  • 20 is an int, 3.1416 a float, "Ada" a str.
  • "20" with quotes is text. int("20") turns it into the number.
  • ** is a power: radius ** 2 is radius squared. radius * 2 is just double.
  • input() always gives back text, so wrap it: int(input(…)).
