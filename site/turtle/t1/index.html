First steps with the turtle

Two lines of Python give you a turtle: import turtle and t = turtle.Pen(). It sits in the middle of the screen, facing right, pen on the paper. Every command you type, it does at once.

t. Telling the turtle what to do

t.forward(100) t.left(90)
  • t.forward(100) walks 100 steps and draws a line. t.backward(50) walks back.
  • t.left(90) and t.right(90) turn on the spot. 90 is a square corner; 360 is all the way round.
  • t.penup() lifts the pen so the turtle walks without drawing; t.pendown() puts it back.

= Variables are labels

side = 100 t.forward(side)
  • A variable is a name stuck on a value. Change side once at the top and every t.forward(side) changes.
  • 100 / 3 is 33.333…, a decimal. 100 // 3 is 33, a whole number.
  • side * side is the square's area; print(area) shows it under the drawing.
