Turtle playground

Starting Python…

Python's turtle, running in the browser. A turtle starts in the middle of the screen; t.forward(100) walks it 100 steps and draws a line, t.left(90) turns it. Loops make shapes, lists make colours, and a mistake is something you can see. Every script here is ordinary Python and runs unchanged in IDLE.

pyAn editor

Write a whole script with syntax colouring and Tab completion. Run draws it, Step runs one line at a time, Replay plays the drawing again, Save picture downloads an SVG.

✓Checks that read the drawing

A task's check looks at what the code drew: how many lines, how long, which way it turned, whether the turtle came home. A miss gets a hint about the picture, never the answer.

A loop with no way out is stopped after a few thousand steps with its drawing so far, and anything still running after 5 seconds is killed and Python restarted.

How it works

Real PythonCPython 3.12 compiled for the browser (Pyodide) runs in a background thread. Nothing is installed and nothing is sent to a server.
The real turtle modulebasthon-turtle, a rewrite of Python's turtle that draws vector graphics instead of a window. Same commands as IDLE.
Every command recordedEach turtle action is stamped with the line of code that made it. That is what the stepper lights, what Replay plays, and what the checks measure.
Lessons on topThe same pieces, with points and a hand-in report: Loops and First steps so far.

    Maze Runner

    Get the turtle to the red flag. Click the picture and use the arrow keys (↑ forward 20, ← → turn 15, ↓ back), or type commands at the prompt: t.forward(100) moves further in one go. Walls stop the turtle. Fewest moves wins. Levels: t = maze(2), t = maze(3).

    Mazet = maze(1)
    >>>
    💰

    Treasure Hunt

    Treasure is buried at a secret spot. After every move the game says warmer or colder. Use the arrow keys, or jump with t.goto(x, y) (the numbers round the edge are the coordinates). When it says very hot, t.dig().

    Huntt = treasure()
    >>>
    🎯

    Cannon

    The target sits somewhere on the ring, at a secret angle. Turn the cannon with t.left(…) and t.right(…) (or ← → for 5° at a time), then t.fire() (or ↑). A miss tells you how far off, and which way. Three shots.

    Aimt = cannon()
    >>>

    Turtle Race

    Four turtles, one finish line, random steps. Pick a program: Race!, or bet first with race("blue"). Five races runs them in a loop and counts the wins in a dictionary.

    Turtle Golf

    The ball is a turtle. Every ball.forward(…) and every turn is a stroke; the fewest strokes into the hole wins. Water sends the ball back to the tee with a penalty. Hole 1 is a straight shot; holes 2 and 3 need a turn or two. Play at the prompt, or write the whole round in the editor and press Run.

    Courseball = golf(1)

    Hints: ball.towards(200, 140) is the heading that points at a spot, and ball.distance(200, 140) how far it is. Both are free (no stroke).

    >>>

    Art Machine

    art() draws a random spirograph and tells you its number, so art(7) gets the same picture again. The editor has the recipe written out: change the sides, the size, the turn and the colours to make your own.
