Dictionaries Lab

A list is a numbered shelf: you find things by their position. A dictionary is a sticker album with a label on every page: you find things by their label, called a key.

[ ] List: numbered

books = ['Holes', 'Matilda']
  • Find by position: books[0]
  • Add to the end: books.append('Wonder')
  • Remove by position: del books[1]

{ } Dictionary: labelled

pets = {'Kai': 'lizard', 'Mo': 'frog'}
  • Look up by key: pets['Kai']
  • Change or add: pets['Mo'] = 'toad'
  • Remove by key: del pets['Kai']
  • Count entries: len(pets)
  • No positions: pets[0] is a KeyError.
